RBF.TAXONOMY // 16 ARCHETYPES

A Taxonomy Designed for Builders

Build-in-public is more than just shipping announcements. Releasebuff classifies every commit into four distinct domains of engineering storytelling.

Product Progress

Tangible milestones, system upgrades, and measurable software developments.

1. New feature shipped Net new user capability or major flow
2. Product improvement UX polish, speedup, or refactor
3. Bug fixed Root cause resolution & sanity check
4. Weekly build recap Rollup digest of week's shipped work

Learning & Craft

Engineering depth, architecture decisions, and developer lessons learned in production.

5. Technical trade-off Why we chose SQLite over DynamoDB
6. Lesson learned Hard-won production insight
7. Failed experiment What didn't pan out and why it matters
8. Behind the scenes Tooling setups, terminal dotfiles, workflow

Product Marketing

Framing technical solutions in terms of user value and tangible customer benefits.

9. Product showcase/demo Visual walkthrough of key capabilities
10. Problem & solution Friction breakdown and the resolution
11. Launch promotion Major version release and public debut
12. What's next Transparent roadmap and forward horizon

Audience & Growth

Fostering transparent founder relationships and community feedback loops.

13. Ask the audience Solicit architecture or feature feedback
14. Traction & milestones GitHub stars, active users, latency goals
15. Customer feedback Real user reactions and direct impact
16. Founder journey Reflections on early-stage velocity & grit
